<h3 align="center">Photo ma euta, aayo aarko?</h3>

<p align="center">
  Online shopping in Nepal runs on guesswork. The photo looks one way, the parcel looks another,<br>
  and the size chart is a coin toss.
</p>

<p align="center">
  Drapmi fixes that with one promise: <b>see it on you before you buy.</b><br>

## Your photo. Their clothes.

Upload one photo of yourself. Pick any piece from a Nepali label. See it on you, from the front, the side and the back.

The seller's photo is the truth. The colour, the embroidery and the cut stay as the seller made them. You get one image per try-on, and nothing is made until you ask.

A phone can't tell you how a fabric feels, so sellers say it for you: how heavy it is, whether it stretches, whether it's see-through, how it fits.

## Then watch it turn

A four-second clip of the piece on a person, in one of twelve moods, with music. If a clip doesn't look like you, we remake it free. Every clip carries the seller's name: made with Drapmi x the label.

## For labels

A storefront and a link to share, free to start. Every order arrives with a two-hour clock to confirm it, so shoppers are never left waiting.

You see what shoppers try on, and what they buy. Plans from free to Pro add analytics, reviews and a lower fee on orders that come from your own audience. **Founding labels pay no commission** while the founding programme runs.
